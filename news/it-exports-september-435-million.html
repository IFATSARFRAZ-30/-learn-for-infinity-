Pakistan's IT exports reach $435 million in September

16 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

Pakistan exported about $435 million of IT services in September 2026, up 19% from the same month last year and up 10% from August.

  • September 2026: $435 million
  • July to September (first quarter of FY27): about $1.25 billion
  • Last 12 months: $4.8 billion, up 20% from $4 billion a year earlier

Topline Securities put part of the monthly rise down to a higher number of working days in September.

What it means

IT exports are software and tech services sold to customers abroad, and they bring in dollars. Because a single month can swing on working days alone, the 12-month total is the steadier signal, and that is up about a fifth.

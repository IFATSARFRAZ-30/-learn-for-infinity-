KSE-100 ends the week above 166,000 points

16 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

The Pakistan Stock Exchange benchmark KSE-100 index closed on Friday, 16 October, at 166,419.72 points, a gain of 233.55 points. It followed a 768-point rise on Thursday that had taken the index to 166,186.

  • Friday close: 166,419.72 points
  • Friday change: +233.55 points (about +0.14%)
  • Thursday close: 166,186 points (+768, about +0.46%)

Express Tribune described Friday's advance as selective buying in a handful of large-cap stocks, with activity staying measured around the midday break. The Nation reported that bullish sentiment carried on from the day before.

What it means

A point change only means something next to the index level: 233 points on a 166,000 index is a small move, about one seventh of one percent. The KSE-100 is weighted by company size, so buying in a few of the biggest stocks can move it without most of the market following. When you read "the index rose", check whether the gain was broad or narrow.

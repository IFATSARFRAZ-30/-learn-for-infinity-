IMF and Pakistan agree on a $1.2 billion tranche

8 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

IMF staff and Pakistan reached a staff-level agreement on 8 October on the fourth review of the Extended Fund Facility, which would release about $1.2 billion once the IMF Executive Board approves.

  • Extended Fund Facility: about $1.0 billion
  • Resilience and Sustainability Facility: about $210 million
  • Total disbursed under both arrangements would reach about $5.7 billion

The talks ran from 23 September to 7 October in Karachi and Islamabad. The IMF said gross reserves rose to about $21.5 billion by the end of September and that headline inflation eased to about 10.3% in September after peaking in May, while warning that risks remain high. Commitments include staying within the FY27 budget, reforming tax administration, phasing out the fuel support scheme, keeping monetary policy tight, and adjusting energy tariffs on time. The report gives no date for board approval.

What it means

A staff-level agreement is an understanding between IMF staff and the government. No money moves until the Executive Board approves it, so the date of that vote is the next thing to watch.

FBR extends the 2026 tax return deadline to 31 October

15 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

The Federal Board of Revenue has pushed the deadline for filing Tax Year 2026 returns from 15 October to 31 October. It is the second extension this season.

  • New deadline: 31 October 2026
  • Previous deadline: 15 October 2026
  • Returns filed so far: about 6.9 million

The FBR said it granted the extra time after requests from trade bodies and tax bar associations. The extension covers people who had to file by 30 September, a date that had earlier been moved to 15 October. ProPakistani also reported slowdowns, glitches and bugs during the season, including the IRIS portal failing to properly accept returns for many individuals. The report does not mention penalties.

What it means

If you have not filed yet, the practical advice is to file well before 31 October rather than on the last day, since portals tend to slow down near deadlines. Check the FBR notification for the exact rules that apply to your category.

General information, not tax advice.

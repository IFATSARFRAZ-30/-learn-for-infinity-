WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html, explore.html, news.html, academy.html   replace the 4 files with the same names in your repo
  data.js                                             NEW: all articles, news, courses and videos live here
  site.css, site.js                                   NEW: shared styles and script for the 4 pages
  images/small/                                       NEW: lighter copies of your pictures (about 0.8 MB in total)

DO NOT DELETE ANYTHING ELSE. Your article and news pages, style.css, script.js, psx.html and your existing images stay as they are.

HOW TO UPLOAD ON GITHUB (website, no software needed)
  1. Open your repo, press  Add file  >  Upload files.
  2. Drag in all 7 files and the "images" folder from this folder (the folder keeps its structure).
  3. Type a message such as "New home, explore, news and academy pages" and press Commit changes.
  4. Wait about a minute, then open the site and press Ctrl+F5 (or Cmd+Shift+R) to see it.

HOW TO ADD A NEW ARTICLE OR NEWS ITEM LATER
  1. Make its own page in articles/ or news/ as you do now, and put its picture in images/small/articles/ or images/small/news/.
  2. Open data.js, copy one entry from the right list, paste it at the TOP of that list, and change the words.
  3. Save. The home, explore and news pages all update by themselves, newest first.
  To feature something in the carousels, set  featured:true  on it (trending). Set it back to false later.

Why interest rates affect businesses more than they realise

Drag the rate and watch profit disappear. · 5 min read · interactive

A few points on an interest rate can turn a profitable company into a struggling one.

Imagine a company that earns an operating profit (EBIT) before paying interest. The more it borrows and the higher the rate, the bigger the bite. All figures are in Rs million.

–interest per year
–profit before tax
–interest cover (EBIT ÷ interest)

The ripple effect

  • 1. Loans cost more, so profit shrinks.
  • 2. Customers who borrow (for homes, cars, stock) buy less.
  • 3. Businesses postpone expansion because new projects must beat a higher hurdle.
  • 4. Weak firms struggle to refinance, and some fail.

Test yourself

A firm has Rs 50m of loans. The rate rises from 10% to 20%. How much more interest does it pay each year?

A. 50 × 10% = 5 and 50 × 20% = 10, so the cost doubles.
